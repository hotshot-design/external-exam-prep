# Business quiz: short responses and report skills 2022–2023

Questions adapted from the QCAA Section 1 short-response questions and the checkable skills in the extended-response report (classifying case facts, trends vs relationships, business criteria, calculations from the case data), using the marking guides' sample answers and the syllabus definitions. Case facts are quoted in each question, so you don't need the stimulus book. Each explanation links to the question paper and marking guide, plus the worked solution for 2024–25. Where a guide has no sample answer (for example the 2023 report), classifications follow the syllabus definitions. Source: QCAA external assessments (CC BY 4.0, © State of Queensland (QCAA)).
