# Business – what the 2024 and 2025 papers show

::: tip Source
Analysis of the QCAA **2024 and 2025 Business external assessments** (`snr_business_24_ea_*`, `snr_business_25_ea_*`) including marking guides. QCAA papers are CC BY 4.0, © State of Queensland (QCAA). The case-study business is fictional.

## Paper structure
| | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Total marks | **38** | **41** |
| Section 1: short response | 4 questions, **13** marks | 3 questions, **17** marks |
| Section 2: extended response | 1 report, **25** marks | 1 report, **24** marks |
| Case study | Stimulus 1–11 (charts, plans, financials, stakeholders, costs) | Music Co Pty Ltd, Stimulus 1–11 |
| Time | 15 min planning + 120 min | same |

The report is only **400–600 words**, which is short, so every sentence needs to carry a mark.

## Section 1 questions so far
| Year | Question | Marks | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Explain the importance of both **retraining and development** when transforming a business | 2 | Explain |
| 2024 | Identify and explain one reason businesses use **Kotter rather than Lewin** | 3 | Explain/compare |
| 2024 | Identify and describe one **internal** environmental factor and one **external operating** factor driving change (from the case study) | 4 | Describe |
| 2024 | Using Stimulus 9, identify **two key stakeholders** to manage closely and analyse their impact | 4 | Analyse |
| 2025 | Explain how **competition** can be a driver of change, with one example | 2 | Explain |
| 2025 | Identify and explain **Kotter steps 4, 5 and 6** (Communicate the vision, Empower action, Create quick wins) | 6 | Identify + explain |
| 2025 | **Force field analysis**: two forces *for* change, two forces *against* change (using case data), plus one action to implement change | 9 | Select data, analyse |

**Learn precisely:** all 8 Kotter steps with a one-line explanation of each, Lewin's unfreeze–change–refreeze, drivers of change (internal vs external operating vs external general), and how to run a force field analysis from case data. The sample answers cite stimulus numbers in brackets, e.g. "(Stimulus 7)", so refer to the data.

## The extended report (24–25 marks)
The task is almost identical in both years:

> Prepare a **business report** for the owners using the case study. **Describe** the business situation and conduct a **SWOT**. Identify **a relationship and a trend** from the SWOT, **interpret their effects** on the business situation, and draw **conclusions about the implications** for the transformation. Using **two business criteria**, **evaluate one** strategy to make a **decision** and propose a **recommendation**.

2024 evaluated a **financial management strategy** (and asked for the feasibility of the strategic plan). 2025 evaluated a **change management strategy**. Revise both families of strategies.

### Marks by criterion (2025, 24 marks)
| Criterion | Marks | What gets top marks |
|---|---|---|
| **Describing** | 2 | A *comprehensive* description of the business situation |
| **Analysing** (SWOT) | 4 | Relevant data from the case; each factor **correctly classified** as S/W/O/T; insightful analysis |
| **Synthesising** | 8 | One relationship **and** one trend from the SWOT; discriminating interpretation of their **effects**; insightful synthesis of **implications**; valid conclusions |
| **Evaluating** | 8 | Identify a strategy from the case; use **two business criteria** with perceptive judgments; a **thoroughly justified decision**; an appropriate **recommendation** |
| **Communicating** | 2 (2025) / 3 (2024) | Concise, logical sequencing; in 2024 also *used the business report genre purposefully to suit the audience* |

::: warning Where the marks are
**16 of 24 marks** are Synthesising and Evaluating. A long SWOT earns at most 4. Aim for a short SWOT (about 2 per quadrant, each with a data point), then spend your words on relationship/trend and the evaluation.
:::

### Report template (400–600 words)
1. **Heading, To/From/Date/Subject** (report conventions).
2. **Business situation** (2–3 sentences with figures: age, stage of life cycle, performance trend, strategic goals).
3. **SWOT** (table or bullets; each factor has a data reference).
4. **Relationship and trend**: e.g. "**Relationship:** the cost of R&D (W) limits the ability to exploit streaming (O). **Trend:** retail purchases are forecast to fall while streaming revenue rises." Then *interpret the effect* on the business and state the *implication* for transformation.
5. **Evaluation**: name one strategy from the case, then apply **Criterion 1** and **Criterion 2** with evidence and a judgment on each.
6. **Decision** (go/no-go or modified go) and **Recommendation** (specific action, timing, who).

::: tip Criteria (from the syllabus)
The syllabus defines **four business criteria**: **competitiveness** (meets or exceeds market standards relative to rivals), **effectiveness** (goals and objectives achieved, target problems solved), **efficiency** (optimal use of resources and processes) and **stakeholder satisfaction** (stakeholder expectations considered and managed). Choose **two** that suit the strategy and the case data, and judge the strategy against each ("to what degree…").
:::

## Earlier papers: 2020–2022 (older 2019 syllabus)
Same Unit 4 Topic 2 content, but a different mark structure and objective wording. Everything below is still in scope for 2026.
| Year | Total | Section 1 | Report | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 45 | 21 (6 + 6 + 9) | 24 marks, no word limit | hydroponic herb farm |
| 2021 | 35 | 15 (4 + 3 + 3 + 5) | 20 marks, 400–600 words | surfboard maker (Section 1), vege-meat butcher (report) |
| 2022 | 39 | 20 (3 + 4 + 4 + 9) | 19 marks, 400–600 words | vet clinic |

| Year | Questions |
|---|---|
| 2020 | Legislative compliance (explain two circumstances); two strategic plan objectives; three **refreeze** activities; report analysing **stakeholder power-interest** and evaluating an operations decision |
| 2021 | Management styles as drivers; an environmental factor; when to use a **consultant**; **force field** (two for, two against); report with **SWOT**, trends, evaluation of existing **marketing** strategies |
| 2022 | **Performance management** outcome; a **Kotter** step and culture; a **macro** factor; evaluate one **HR** strategy with two criteria; report analysing **pressures for and against** change plus relationships/trends |

**2023 (35 marks):** Section 1 (10: 3 + 4 + 3) on Lewin's refreeze stage with an example risk, a strategy to overcome resistance to change (e.g. participation), and how an internal operating factor is influenced; then a **25-mark report** (400–600 words) on a hotel company's takeover of an oyster farm: SWOT, **two** relationships/patterns/trends interpreted, and **two criteria** to evaluate one **operational** strategy. Marks: Analysing 6, Synthesising 8, Evaluating 8, Communicating 3. Case stimuli included seasonal revenue, market-share pies, a tourism report and staff ideas. No sample report is published.

**Patterns across 2020–2025**
- **Tools:** SWOT (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025), force field (2021, 2022, 2025), power-interest (2020, 2024).
- **Theories:** Lewin (2020, 2023, and compared with Kotter in 2024), Kotter (2022, 2024, 2025).
- **Function evaluated:** operations (2020, 2023), marketing (2021), HR (2022), financial (2024), change management (2025). A different function each year, so practise **operations, marketing and HR** strategies as well as the two most recent.
- **Criteria:** the 2021 guide lists competitiveness, effectiveness, efficiency and stakeholder satisfaction; later years accept any two.
- **Guide advice (2021):** "evaluate existing practices … not possible alternatives. Decisions should relate to the strategies used to date. Recommendations may include alternative strategies."
- **Short-response marking** is point by point: identify, explain, example, link to the stimulus (about 1 mark each).

## Revision actions
- Re-do 2024 and 2025 Section 1 from memory, then compare to the marking guides (the sample answers show the expected level of detail).
- Time yourself on the report: it is 24–25 of the 38–41 marks, so allow about 65–70 of the 120 working minutes (400–600 words), after 15 minutes of planning. Do it once with each year's case study.
- Use the [worked case](/business/practice) and [glossary](/business/glossary) to rehearse terminology.
