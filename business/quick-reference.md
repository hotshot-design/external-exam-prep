# Business – exam-day quick reference

::: tip Paper (2024–25): 38–41 marks
**Section 1** short response (13–17) + **Section 2 business report, 400–600 words (24–25)**. Unit 4 Topic 2: transformation (renewal). 15 min planning + 120 min; spend about 65–70 min on the report.
:::

## Theories
**Lewin:** unfreeze → change → refreeze. **Kotter (8 steps):** 1 create urgency · 2 build a guiding coalition · 3 form a strategic vision · 4 **communicate the vision** · 5 **empower action** · 6 **create quick wins** · 7 build on the change · 8 make it stick. *Kotter vs Lewin:* Kotter gives practical detail and tackles resistance; Lewin is too simple.

## Four business criteria (syllabus)
| Criterion | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Competitiveness | offers products/services that meet or exceed market standards relative to rivals |
| Effectiveness | goals and objectives achieved; target problems solved |
| Efficiency | optimal use of resources and processes |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | stakeholder expectations considered and managed |

## Drivers and resistance
Drivers: corporate culture, management styles, organisational structures, competition, legislative compliance. Factors: internal, external operating, macro. Resistance: communication, participation, negotiation, manipulation, threat. Styles: autocratic → persuasive → consultative → participative → laissez-faire.

## Force field (Section 1, 9 marks in 2025)
Two forces **for**, two **against**, each with case data and analysis (cite stimulus numbers) + **one action** (e.g. cost-benefit analysis).

## Report skeleton (24–25 marks)
1. **Description** (2): headings, situation with figures.
2. **SWOT** (4): data in the right quadrant (internal S/W, external O/T).
3. **One relationship + one trend**, effects, implications, conclusions (8).
4. **ONE strategy, TWO criteria**, a judgment on each (8): change management (2025) or financial management (2024).
5. **Decision**, then a **recommendation drawn from the decision**.
6. Concise, logical layout (2–3).

*Relationship* = how two SWOT factors affect each other. *Trend* = direction over time.
