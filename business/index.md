## Exam format (QCAA Business syllabus v1.3, p.45)
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | External examination — combination response (**25%**) |
| Content | **Unit 4, Topic 2: Transformation of a business** only |
| Planning time | 15 minutes |
| Working time | 120 minutes |
| Responses | Sentences/paragraphs, plus an **extended response: a business report or report extract** |
| Stimulus | Unseen stimulus (a business case) |

::: tip Scope
The exam relates to **Unit 4 Topic 2 (Transformation – renewal)**. Topic 1 (Repositioning: steady state/decline) is not the exam focus, though it is useful background. The syllabus notes there is **no prescribed case study** for Topic 2, so the case will be unseen.
:::

::: info In the 2024 and 2025 papers
**38 and 41 marks**: Section 1 short response on drivers of change, Kotter/Lewin and force field analysis (13–17 marks), then one **400–600 word business report** (24–25 marks) with SWOT, relationship/trend, and an evaluation using **two business criteria**. See [past papers analysis](/business/past-papers).
:::

## Assessment objectives
1. **Describe** a business situation and environment(s) relating to transformation in the post-maturity stage.
2. **Explain** business concepts and strategies relating to transformation.
3. **Analyse and interpret** situations, environments and key business functions using an analytical tool (SWOT, force field).
4. **Evaluate** transformation strategies to make a decision and propose recommendation(s).
