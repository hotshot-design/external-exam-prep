# Spanish – exam-day quick reference

::: tip Paper (2024–25): 52–53 marks
**Section 1** audio, answer in **Spanish** (14–15) · **Section 2** two written texts, answer in **English** (17) · **Section 3** **email in Spanish, 200–300 words** (21). 5 min perusal + 120 min. The audio can be replayed as often as needed.
:::

## The "conclusion + N examples" rule
State the answer (1 mark), then give **exactly N specific examples** from the text (1 mark each). Section 1 also marks your Spanish (4 = few errors).

| Asked | Say | Spanish frame (Section 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Context | the situation, who, why | *El contexto es… Esto es evidente cuando…* |
| Purpose | what the speaker/writer wants | *El propósito es… Primero…, además…* |
| Audience | who it is for, plus evidence (address, topics) | *La audiencia es… Se nota cuando…* |
| Tone | one adjective + examples of language | *El tono es [agradecido]. Lo demuestra cuando…* |
| Perspective | a viewpoint on the topic + 2 examples | *Una perspectiva es que… Por ejemplo…* |

Tone words: agradecido, emocionado, orgulloso, nostálgico, optimista, formal, urgente, persuasivo, entusiasta.

## Email checklist (Section 3)
- [ ] **Greeting** (*Estimado/a…*, *¡Hola, Profe!*) and **sign-off** (*Atentamente*, *Un saludo*): 2 marks
- [ ] **Every bullet** addressed **and developed** in its own paragraph
- [ ] **Register** consistent (*usted* with a teacher or hotel)
- [ ] A **range of tenses**: preterite/imperfect, present, future/conditional, and one subjunctive (*espero que… / cuando termine…*)
- [ ] Linkers: *sin embargo, además, por eso, aunque, por último*
- [ ] **200–300 words**; accents checked

## Tense chooser
Completed past: preterite · background or habit: imperfect · plans: *ir a* + infinitive or future · polite wishes: conditional (*me gustaría*) · wishes and doubts: present subjunctive · unreal *si*: imperfect subjunctive + conditional.

## Unit 4 topics (Mi presente; mi futuro)
finishing school and graduation · post-school options (study, work, gap year, travel) · independence · volunteering · technology and sustainability · the role of Spanish (study, careers, travel) · global citizenship.
