# Spanish

**Exam:** Wed 28 Oct, PM · [Timetable](/timetable)

## Exam format (QCAA Spanish syllabus v1.3, pp.34–35)
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | External examination — combination response (**25%**) |
| Content | **Unit 4: Mi presente; mi futuro** |
| Perusal | 5 minutes |
| Working time | 120 minutes |
| Stimulus | 3–6 authentic Spanish texts, up to 1000 words total, at least one written and one **audio/audiovisual** |
| Responses (syllabus) | Short answers in **English** (up to 100 words); short answers in **Spanish** (up to 100 words); **extended responses in Spanish** (up to 400 words). *In the 2024 and 2025 papers the extended response was a 200–300 word email.* |
| Not allowed | Notes, dictionaries, reference materials |

May ask you to interpret graphs/tables/diagrams, respond to unseen stimulus, and analyse, synthesise and evaluate.

::: info In the 2024 and 2025 papers
**52–53 marks**: Section 1 audio, answered in **Spanish** (14–15); Section 2 two written texts, answered in **English** (17); Section 3 an **email of 200–300 words** in Spanish (21, including 2 marks for a greeting and sign-off or formal opening and closure). Short answers ask for a conclusion plus a set number of examples. See [past papers analysis](/spanish/past-papers).
:::

## Assessment objectives
1. Comprehend Spanish (information, ideas, opinions, experiences).
2. Identify **tone, purpose, context, audience** to infer meaning.
3. **Analyse and evaluate** to draw conclusions.
4. Apply knowledge of **language elements** to construct meaning.
5. **Structure, sequence, synthesise** to justify opinions.
6. Communicate in contextually appropriate Spanish (short and extended).

## Unit 4 topics (what stimulus will be about)
- **The present:** end-of-school life, graduation (graduación, formatura, fiesta de fin de curso) in Australia and Spanish-speaking communities; young people shaping the future (technology, sustainability, innovation); social and global events; **volunteering**.
- **Future choices:** employment prospects, benefits of speaking a second language; post-school options and independence; the role of Spanish in future study, travel, careers; being a **global citizen**, staying connected to friends.
