# Spanish quiz: tenses and grammar

Multiple choice and fill-in questions on the tenses that earn marks in the extended response. **Note:** fill-in answers ignore accents in this quiz tool, so check your accents separately (see [verbs and tenses](/spanish/verbs)).

## Choose the correct form

:::quiz
question: Cuando ___ el colegio, viajaré a Argentina. (future meaning)
answer-correct: termine
answer: termino
answer: terminé
answer: terminaba
explanation: After cuando with a future meaning, use the present subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Si ___ más tiempo, haría voluntariado.
answer-correct: tuviera
answer: tendría
answer: tengo
answer: tuve
explanation: Si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: El año pasado ___ como voluntaria.
answer-correct: trabajé
answer: trabajo
answer: trabajaba
answer: trabajaré
explanation: A completed past event takes the preterite.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Mientras yo ___, mi hermano jugaba al fútbol.
answer-correct: estudiaba
answer: estudié
answer: estudio
answer: estudiaré
explanation: Background action in the past: imperfect.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Espero que tú ___ venir.
answer-correct: puedas
answer: puedes
answer: podrás
answer: pudiste
explanation: "Espero que" triggers the present subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Mañana nosotros ___ temprano.
answer-correct: saldremos
answer: salimos
answer: salíamos
answer: salieron
explanation: Future of salir: saldr- + endings.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Me gustaría que ___ conmigo.
answer-correct: vinieras
answer: vengas
answer: vienes
answer: viniste
explanation: Me gustaría que + imperfect subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Es importante que los jóvenes ___ a la comunidad.
answer-correct: ayuden
answer: ayudan
answer: ayudarán
answer: ayudaron
explanation: Impersonal expression of importance → subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Creo que el voluntariado ___ importante.
answer-correct: es
answer: sea
answer: fue
answer: será
explanation: "Creo que" takes the indicative; "no creo que" takes the subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: No creo que ___ fácil.
answer-correct: sea
answer: es
answer: fue
answer: será
explanation: Doubt or denial → subjunctive.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Estoy nervioso porque mañana ___ mi examen.
answer-correct: es
answer: soy
answer: estoy
answer: fue
explanation: Ser for events (the exam is tomorrow); estar for temporary states (estoy nervioso).
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: "Llevo dos años aprendiendo español" means…
answer-correct: I have been learning Spanish for two years
answer: I took two years to learn Spanish
answer: I will learn Spanish in two years
answer: I wear Spanish
explanation: llevar + time + gerund: have been doing for.
shuffle: true
:::

:::quiz
question: Which is correct?
answer-correct: Voy a estudiar medicina
answer: Voy a estudio medicina
answer: Voy estudiar medicina
answer: Voy a estudiando medicina
explanation: Ir a + infinitive.
shuffle: true
:::

## Fill in the form

:::quiz
question: Complete: Llevo cinco años ___ español (estudiar).
answer-input: estudiando
explanation: Gerund: llevar + time + -ando.
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete: Cuando ___ (terminar) el colegio, viajaré.
answer-input: termine
explanation: Present subjunctive after cuando with future meaning.
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete: Si ___ (tener, yo) más tiempo, haría voluntariado.
answer-input: tuviera
explanation: Imperfect subjunctive of tener (ellos tuvieron → tuviera).
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete: Es importante que ellos ___ (ayudar).
answer-input: ayuden
explanation: Present subjunctive of ayudar.
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete: Ayer ellos ___ (hacer) un proyecto de reciclaje.
answer-input: hicieron
explanation: Preterite of hacer.
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete the future: Mañana nosotros ___ (salir) temprano.
answer-input: saldremos
explanation: Irregular future stem saldr-.
:::

:::quiz
question: Complete: Mientras yo ___ (estudiar), mi hermano jugaba.
answer-input: estudiaba
explanation: Imperfect for background.
:::
