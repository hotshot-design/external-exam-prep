## Exam format (QCAA General Mathematics syllabus v1.3, pp.43–45)
| | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | **30%** of final mark | **20%** of final mark |
| Questions | Short response + multiple choice, **simple familiar** | Short response, **complex familiar** and **complex unfamiliar** |
| Perusal | 5 min | 5 min |
| Working time | 90 min | 90 min |
| Content | Units 3 and 4 (+ assumed knowledge from Units 1–2) | Units 3 and 4 (+ assumed knowledge from Units 1–2) |

- QCAA provides the **General Mathematics formula book**.
- You may use a **handheld QCAA-approved scientific calculator** (scientific only, so be fluent with recurrence and formula methods). No notes or other resources.

::: info In the 2023–2025 papers
Paper 1 is **57 marks** (15 MC + 10 short response); Paper 2 is **38 marks** (7 short response). Networks, finance, time and location, bivariate data and sequences dominate. See [past papers analysis](/maths/past-papers).
:::

## Overall mark allocation (± 2%)
| Difficulty | Share | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Simple familiar | 60% | Objectives 1, 2, 3 (recall, use, communicate) |
| Complex familiar | 20% | Any objective |
| Complex unfamiliar | 20% | Objectives 4, 5, 6 (evaluate reasonableness, justify, solve) |

## Assessment objectives
1. Recall mathematical knowledge · 2. Use mathematical knowledge · 3. Communicate mathematical knowledge · 4. Evaluate the reasonableness of solutions · 5. Justify procedures and decisions · 6. Solve mathematical problems.

## Content covered
**Unit 3:** Bivariate data analysis · Time series analysis · Growth and decay in sequences · Earth geometry and time zones
**Unit 4:** Loans, investments and annuities · Graphs and networks · Networks and decision mathematics
