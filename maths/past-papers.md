## Paper structure
| | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Marks | **57** | **38** |
| Section 1 | 15 multiple choice (15 marks) | 7 short response (38 marks, no MC) |
| Section 2 | 10 short response (42 marks) | |
| Marks per question | MC 1 each; short 3–5 marks | 4, 5, 5, 5, 7, 6, 6 (2025) |
| Perusal + working | 5 + 90 min | 5 + 90 min |
| Aids | Scientific calculator, formula book | same |

**Pacing:** Paper 1 is about **1.6 minutes per mark** (90 min ÷ 57 marks): do the 15 multiple-choice questions in about 20 minutes, leaving about 70 minutes for the 42 short-response marks. Paper 2 is about **2.4 minutes per mark** (90 ÷ 38), so the 5–7 mark questions deserve 12–17 minutes each. Use the 5 minutes of perusal to find the networks and finance questions.

**Highest-yield subtopics.** *Every year:* Hungarian algorithm, minimum spanning tree, reducing-balance loan, least-squares prediction and interpretation, time zones, walk/trail/path/cycle terms, correlation vs causation. *Five of six years:* critical path and float, seasonal index and deseasonalising. *Four of six:* perpetuity, flow and minimum cut. *Often:* effective annual rate comparisons, geometric growth/decay, arithmetic depreciation.

**Format:** Paper 1 is 57 marks and Paper 2 is 38 marks in 2021–25 (95 total). In 2020 the papers were out of 60 and 40 (100 total). Times were always 5 + 90 minutes per paper.

## Question styles
- **Paper 1 simple familiar:** "Determine", "Calculate", "Write a recurrence relation", "Name the location". Short, mostly procedural.
- **Paper 2 complex:** a story with a data table, then *"Use a weighted graph / flow network diagram / scatterplot to … determine … justify"*. You must **produce a representation** (graph, diagram, plot) and then **reason from it**. The question names the representation to use. If you skip it, you lose method marks.
- **Justify / explain whether**: e.g. "Justify whether she completed her walk in under 15 minutes", "Determine if the value will be lower than one third". The final line must be a **yes/no with numbers**.
- **Questions worth more than one mark require working**: mathematical reasoning must be shown.

## Command terms (as used in these papers)
**Determine • Calculate • Justify • Explain • Display • Provide a justified decision • Use [a diagram] to determine • Write a recurrence relation • Identify**.
"Justify" and "provide a justified decision" need a number or statistic *and* a conclusion in words.

## Practice routine
1. Do **Paper 1 MC 2025** in 20 minutes. Check against the guide and categorise each miss by topic.
2. Do **Paper 2 2025** under timed conditions. Spend extra time on the networks and sequences questions.
3. Redo any question you lost marks on, then write the method steps next to the guide's solution.
4. Use [worked examples](/maths/worked-examples) for the same topics and the [glossary](/maths/glossary) for definitions.

## Calculator and formula book reminders
- Use degree mode for trigonometry and bearings.
- Only a scientific calculator is allowed, so for finance be fluent with the recurrence form (next = previous × (1 + r) − payment) and with the formula-book formulas.
- Practise *finding* the formulas you need in the formula book quickly, and check which ones it actually provides.
- Round only at the end, and give units (km, \$, minutes).
