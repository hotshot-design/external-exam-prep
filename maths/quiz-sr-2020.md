# Maths quiz: short responses 2020

Type-in questions adapted from the QCAA short-response and Paper 2 questions (every answer recomputed; diagram-only questions are left out and some are split into steps). Each explanation links to the question paper, the marking guide and the full **worked solution**, which has the working and mark-by-mark criteria. Single-answer questions are checked with **Check answer**. Source: QCAA external assessments (CC BY 4.0, © State of Queensland (QCAA)).

question: The y-values 4, 16, 8, p and q have mean 11, and q = p + 3. What is p?
answer-input: 12
explanation: p + q = 55 − 28 = 27, so 2p + 3 = 27 → p = 12 (q = 15).
