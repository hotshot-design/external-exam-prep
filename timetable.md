## Exam-day basics

::: warning The QCAA timetable PDF has no exam-day rules
The 2026 QCAA document gives dates, sessions and times only. It states that clashes within a session move to the other session on the same day, that most students sit at most two exams a day, and that school texts and topic confirmations are on the QCAA Student Portal. The points below are **general advice**, so check your school's exam notice and the Student Portal for arrival time, ID, permitted equipment, and the illness/adverse-circumstances process.
:::

- Arrive early for your session (confirm the time with your school).
- Use perusal time to **read the paper** (check what you may do with a pen during perusal).
- Bring only equipment permitted for each subject: Maths and Digital Solutions allow an approved scientific calculator, and Maths provides a formula book (per the syllabuses).
- Clash or illness: speak to your school immediately.
