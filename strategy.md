# Exam strategy

## In the week before
- Use the [quizzes](/quizzes) for quick recall checks, but do **timed past papers** for exam practice.
- Sleep 8+ hours; stop heavy study the night before.
- Do timed past-paper questions rather than only re-reading notes.
- Rotate subjects: one main block, one lighter block per day.

## In the exam
- Use perusal to read every question and pick where to start.
- Allocate time by marks, using the paper's own numbers (from the 2024–25 papers):

| Subject | Marks | Working time | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | 45 | 120 min (+15 plan) | One 800–1000 word essay: about 20 min per body paragraph |
| Spanish | 52–53 | 120 min (+5 perusal) | About 25 min Section 1, 35 Section 2, 50 Section 3 email, 10 checking (a suggested split) |
| Business | 38–41 | 120 min (+15 plan) | Section 1 about 35 min; the 24–25 mark report about 65–70 min |
| Film | 32–35 | 120 min (+20 plan) | One 800–1000 word essay with contexts, 2–3 analysis paragraphs |
| General Maths P1 | 57 | 90 min (+5) | About 1.6 min per mark; MC in about 20 min |
| General Maths P2 | 38 | 90 min (+5) | About 2.4 min per mark |
| Digital Solutions | 66–67 | 120 min (+5) | About 1.8 min per mark |
- Read the command term (analyse, evaluate, justify…) and answer that task exactly.
- Leave 5–10 minutes at the end to check.

## Between exams
- Short walk, food and water; avoid post-mortems of the paper you just sat.

## QCAA command terms

::: warning From general knowledge
The official list is the QCAA cognitive verbs glossary: <https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/senior-qce/common/snr_glossary_cognitive_verbs.pdf>. Check wording there.
:::

| Term | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Describe** | Give characteristics and features |
| **Explain** | Make clear with reasons or causes |
| **Identify** | Recognise and name |
| **Interpret** | Draw meaning from information |
| **Analyse** | Break into parts and examine relationships |
| **Compare** | Show similarities and differences |
| **Justify** | Give reasons or evidence to support a decision |
| **Evaluate** | Judge value or effectiveness using criteria |
| **Recommend / propose** | Put forward a course of action with support |
| **Synthesise** | Combine ideas to form something new |
