# English – exam-day quick reference

::: tip One page to read before you walk in
Format (2024–25): **8 texts, choose one text and one of 2 questions, one analytical essay, 800–1000 words, 45 marks, 15 min planning + 120 min writing.**
:::

## Question types (Othello so far)
| Type | Wording | Used for Othello |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | "How is the reader invited to view the concept of X?" / "What comment does the play make about X?" / statement + "Discuss" | jealousy (2024), reputation (2025) |
| Significance | "Analyse the significance of …" (object, place, event, relationship or character) | handkerchief (2024), Emilia (2025) |
| Likely next | race and otherness, trust, deception, love and violence, honour and revenge, gender and power | Iago's soliloquies, Desdemona, Cassio, Bianca, Brabantio, Cyprus, the storm |

## 15-minute plan
1. Choose in 3 minutes. Underline the **key word**.
2. Thesis in one sentence that uses the key word and takes a position.
3. Three or four paragraph ideas, each with 2–3 **short quotes**, a **technique**, an **effect on the reader**, and a **context/value**.
4. A complication or counter-view; conclusion that **synthesises**.

## What the marking guide rewards
**Knowledge application** (interpretation of perspectives/representations; how cultural assumptions, attitudes, values and beliefs underpin the text; how the writer's choices shape the text) · **Organisation and development** (discriminating thesis developed across the essay; well-considered evidence used explicitly; logical sequencing; cohesive devices) · **Textual features** (range of accurate sentence structures; discriminating vocabulary and register; accurate punctuation and spelling).

## Paragraph formula
Topic sentence with prompt key word → embedded quote (6–10 words) → **named technique** → **effect on audience** → context/value → link back to thesis.

## Devices to name
soliloquy, dramatic irony, imagery (animal, light/dark, monster), inversion, repetition, verse to prose, antithesis, foil, juxtaposition, personification, symbol.

## Checks in the last 10 minutes
Names spelled (Desdemona, Cassio, Brabantio, Roderigo, Emilia), quotes accurate, every paragraph links to the question, no plot retelling.
