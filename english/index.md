# English – Othello

**Exam:** Tue 27 Oct, AM · [Timetable](/timetable)

## Exam format (QCAA General English syllabus v1.3, p.47)
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | External examination — extended response (**25%** of final mark) |
| Task | One **analytical essay** answering an **unseen question** on your literary text (Othello) |
| Planning time | 15 minutes |
| Working time | 120 minutes |
| Allowed in | No text, no notes |
| Relates to | Unit 4 (Close study of literary texts) |

The essay must communicate "an informed and critical perspective" to an audience with a deep understanding of the text.

::: info In the 2024 and 2025 papers
**45 marks**, **800–1000 words**, 8 texts with 2 questions each (a concept question or an "Analyse the significance of…" question). See [past papers analysis](/english/past-papers).
:::

## Assessment objectives for the exam
1. Use patterns and conventions of an **analytical essay** for an unseen question.
2. Establish and maintain the **role of essay writer** and relationship with readers.
3. Analyse **perspectives and representations** of concepts, identities, times and/or places.
4. Analyse how **cultural assumptions, attitudes, values and beliefs** underpin the text and invite audiences to take up positions.
5. Analyse the effects of **aesthetic features and stylistic devices**.
6. **Select and synthesise** subject matter to support perspectives.
7. **Organise and sequence** subject matter for purpose.
8. Use **cohesive devices** to emphasise ideas and connect parts.
9. Make **language choices** for purpose.
10. Use **grammar and language structures** for purpose.
11. Use **written features** (spelling, punctuation) for purpose.
