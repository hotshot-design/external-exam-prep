# Film, TV & New Media – what the 2020–2025 papers show

::: tip Source
Analysis of the QCAA **2024 and 2025 Film, Television & New Media external assessments** (`snr_ftvnm_24_ea_*`, `snr_ftvnm_25_ea_*` in the [paper files](/film/papers)), including the 2025 marking guide. ## The paper in numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Marks | **35** (2020, 2024), **37** (2021, 2022, 2023), **32** (2025) |
| Task | Choose **one of three questions**, each with its own stimulus (a short video) |
| Length | **800–1000 words**, analytical essay |
| Time | 20 min planning/viewing + 120 min writing |
| Stimulus | A 1–3.5 minute clip (about 1:16 to 3:30 across the six) with a paragraph of **context** (who made it, when, why, who for) |

## Question patterns
Nearly every question has the same shape (2024 Q3 asks "how has the advertising agency created an effective marketing campaign…?"): **"How effectively …? Justify your viewpoint by analysing [and appraising] the stimulus, also considering the contexts of production and use [including the target audience]."**

| Year | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | **Representations**: *Silent* (Moonbot, animated short about picture and sound) | **Technologies**: *Jurassic World: Dominion* chase scene (tension, positioning) | **Institutions**: Goldfish AR Snapchat lens (marketing to teenagers) |
| 2025 | **Technologies**: Mitski music video *Love Me More* | **Representations**: animation *The Last Job on Earth* (the future) | **Audiences**: Apple web film *The Underdogs* (positioning toward the brand) |

### Takeaways
- **Each question is tied to one key concept**: Representations, Technologies, Institutions or Audiences. The fifth concept, **Languages** (codes and conventions), is also your analysis toolkit; it was the concept of 2021 Q3, 2022 Q2 and 2023 Q1, but not in 2024–25.
- **Mixed media**: animation, feature-film scene, advertisement, music video, web film, AR/social media. Do not revise only "movies".
- Stimulus types are **non-traditional** (new media, ads, B2B content). Know how **institutions** (studios, agencies, platforms, funders) shape content.
- The **context paragraph** hands you production facts (funder, publication venue, audience age, campaign aim, platform). Use these facts in your "context of production and use" paragraphs, since they are what the **Explaining** criterion rewards.
- "**How effectively**" demands a **verdict** (an evaluation), not description.

## 2020–2022 papers (older 2019 syllabus)
Same format (3 clips, choose 1, 800–1000 words, 20 + 120 min) but older marking (see below). Stems ended "Justify your viewpoint by analysing and appraising the stimulus."
| Year | Stimulus | Concept (inferred from the stem) | Stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | *Son of Rambow* (2007) | Technologies | To what extent have technologies been used to create meaning? |
| 2020 | *Up*, "Married life" (Pixar, 2009) | Representations | How effectively has Pixar represented people, places, events, ideas and/or emotions to communicate meaning? |
| 2020 | *Say Hello to Rang-tan* (Greenpeace/Iceland Foods, 2018) | Institutions | Was the institution responsible for monitoring advertising in the UK justified in banning it? |
| 2021 | *The Look* (Procter & Gamble, 2019) | Representations | How effectively has the director represented people, places, events, ideas and/or emotions? |
| 2021 | *Back to the Start* (Chipotle, 2012) | Audiences | How effectively is the audience positioned to make meaning? |
| 2021 | *We've All Been There* (Friends of the Earth, 2020) | Languages | To what extent does the advertisement manipulate technical and symbolic codes to communicate meaning? |
| 2022 | *Timeless* (Lacoste, 2017) | Audiences | How effectively has Lacoste appealed to its target audience? |
| 2022 | "When She Loved Me", *Toy Story 2* (Pixar, 1999) | Languages | How effectively have moving-image media languages been manipulated to evoke an emotional response? |
| 2022 | *The Jungle Book* trailer (Disney, 2016) | Institutions | Was the Australian Classification Board justified in its classification? |

**Patterns across 2020–2025 (concept; 2020–23 papers don't label it, so it is inferred from the stem):** Representations 5, Audiences 4, Technologies 3, Institutions 3, Languages 3. 2023 had no Technologies or Institutions question, so both are plausible for 2026. Two Institutions questions (2020, 2022) asked whether a regulator or classifier was **justified**, so be ready for a verdict-style stem about regulation, classification or banning. Stimuli are mostly advertisements and public-service films, plus Pixar/animation and music video.

**2023** (37 marks): Q1 Languages, *The Grand Budapest Hotel* (Wes Anderson, 2014): how effectively have moving-image media languages been manipulated to create mood? Q2 Audiences, Ted Baker *Mission Impeccable* (2016): how effectively does the campaign encourage **audience participation and sustained engagement** with the brand (clickable characters, a QR code in shop windows, social media rewards)? Q3 Representations, Google Maps *Saroo Brierley: Homeward Bound* (2013): how effectively has it constructed representations of **hope and determination**? 2023's marks were 9 Explaining (key-concept characteristics 3, production 3, use 3), 13 Analysing, 10 Appraising and 5 Written literacy.

**Marking in 2020–23 vs now:** the 2020–23 guides had four criteria (Explaining, Analysing, Appraising, Written literacy), with Explaining including **characteristics of the key concept** as well as production and use contexts, and terminology scored separately in Written literacy. The 2025 guide dropped the key-concept characteristics mark and scores written literacy as one 0–3 scale. Still useful: define the key concept in one sentence in your introduction. Totals were 35, 37 and 37 (2020–22). No sample essays appear in any year's guide.

## What the marking guide rewards (2025, 32 marks)
| Criterion | Marks | Top-level descriptors |
|---|---|---|
| **Explaining** (contexts of production **and** use) | 3 + 3 | *Accurate and detailed* description of **production** context and, separately, **use** context |
| **Analysing** | 5 + 4 + 4 | Identifies/describes **relevant elements** of the stimulus *with accuracy and detail*; identifies **significant interrelationships** between elements; gives **insightful and plausible interpretations** of how elements communicate meaning |
| **Appraising** | 5 + 5 | An **insightful viewpoint** supported by valid and convincing conclusions; **pertinent evidence from the stimulus** used consistently |
| **Applying written literacy skills** | 3 | Sustained, organised, cohesive; terminology and conventions used consistently and with clarity |

That is roughly **6 marks context, 13 analysis, 10 appraisal, 3 writing**. Appraisal and analysis are 23 of 32.

### Turn that into an essay structure
1. **Intro:** viewpoint in one sentence ("The animation effectively …, though …"), name the concept.
2. **Context of production** paragraph (who made it, why, funding, platform, technology available).
3. **Context of use** paragraph (where it was shown, who the audience is, how it is used, outcome).
4. **2–3 analysis paragraphs**, each on one *relationship* between elements (e.g. lighting + music + editing rhythm), then an **effectiveness judgment** ("this is effective because …, for this audience").
5. **Conclusion**: restate the verdict and weigh strengths vs limits.

::: tip "Interrelationships"
The guide's level 4 and 5 wording is about **how elements work together**. Write "The slow dolly-in combines with the muted score to …", not three separate lists of techniques.
:::

## Practice routine (you can do this tonight)
1. Choose one stimulus from each year (the reference lists give YouTube links). Watch twice without notes, then once with notes.
2. In 20 minutes write a plan: the verdict, 3 key elements and how they interrelate, and the production and use facts from the context box.
3. Write a **300-word mini-essay** (one analysis paragraph plus context sentence), or the whole 800–1000 words if you have time.
4. Mark yourself with the table above. Ask your teacher to check a full essay.
