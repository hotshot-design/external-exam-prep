## Exam format (QCAA Film, Television & New Media syllabus v1.4, p.40)
| | |
|---|---|
| Type | External examination — extended response (**25%**) |
| Task | Respond to an **unseen question** (choice from options) and **unseen stimulus**, as an **analytical essay expressing a viewpoint** |
| Skills | Analyse **and** evaluate to answer fully |
| Stimulus | Succinct unseen stimulus with contextual information |
| Planning time | 20 minutes |
| Working time | 120 minutes |

::: info In the 2024 and 2025 papers
**35 and 32 marks**: pick one of **three** questions, each with its own stimulus clip. Each asks *"How effectively …?"*, tied to Representations, Technologies, Institutions or Audiences. **800–1000 words.** See [past papers analysis](/film/past-papers).
:::

## Assessment objectives for the exam
Syllabus numbering (exam): 
4. Apply **written literacy skills** using relevant terminology and language conventions.
5. **Analyse** the use of key concepts and **contexts of production and use** in moving-image media products.
6. **Evaluate** production practices of moving-image media artists to communicate **conceptual, technical or creative purpose**.

*The marking guides split this into four criteria:* **Explaining** the contexts of production and use (6 marks in 2025), **Analysing** the stimulus, **Appraising** (viewpoint and evidence) and **Applying written literacy skills**.

## Five key concepts (syllabus)
- **Technologies** – the tools and processes used to create, distribute and access moving-image media
- **Representations** – constructions of people, places, events, ideas and emotions
- **Audiences** – the people products are made for, who make meanings when they use them
- **Institutions** – the organisations and people whose practices enable or constrain production and use
- **Languages** – systems of signs and symbols organised through codes and conventions

The 2024–25 questions each targeted one of **Technologies, Representations, Institutions or Audiences**.
