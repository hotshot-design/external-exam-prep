# Film quiz: key concepts and the exam

Check the five key concepts, the 2020–2025 question patterns and what the marking guide rewards. See the [quick reference](/film/quick-reference) and [past papers analysis](/film/past-papers).

:::quiz
question: Match each key concept to its definition
pair: Technologies => Tools and processes used to create, distribute and access moving-image media
pair: Representations => Constructions of people, places, events, ideas and emotions
pair: Audiences => People products are made for, who make meanings when they use them
pair: Institutions => Organisations whose practices enable or constrain production and use
pair: Languages => Systems of signs and symbols organised through codes and conventions
:::
