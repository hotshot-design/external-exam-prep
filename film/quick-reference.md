# Film, TV & New Media – exam-day quick reference

::: tip Paper (2024–25): 32–35 marks
Choose **one of three** questions, each with its own unseen clip and context box. **800–1000 word analytical essay with a viewpoint.** 20 min planning + 120 min writing.
:::

## Five key concepts
**Technologies** (tools and processes) · **Representations** (constructions of people, places, events, ideas, emotions) · **Audiences** (people products are made for, who make meanings) · **Institutions** (organisations whose practices enable or constrain production and use) · **Languages** (signs and symbols organised through codes and conventions).

## The question template
*"How effectively … ? Justify your viewpoint by analysing [and appraising] the stimulus, also considering the contexts of production and use [including the target audience]."*

## Marking criteria (2025: 32 marks)
| Criterion | Marks | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| Explaining contexts of production **and** use | 3 + 3 | accurate and detailed facts for each |
| Analysing | 5 + 4 + 4 | accurate elements, significant **interrelationships**, plausible interpretations |
| Appraising | 5 + 5 | insightful viewpoint, pertinent evidence from the clip |
| Written literacy | 3 | organised, cohesive, accurate terminology |

## 20-minute plan
1. Read all three questions and context boxes; choose.
2. Watch twice; list camera, mise-en-scène, sound, editing, graphics, technology.
3. Write the **viewpoint** in one sentence.
4. List 3 **interrelationships** (e.g. low light + silence + slow cutting).
5. List 3 production facts and 3 use facts from the box, each tied to the concept.

## Essay shape
Intro (viewpoint) → production context → use context → 2–3 analysis paragraphs (evidence, interrelationship, meaning, **effectiveness judgment**) → conclusion (verdict).
