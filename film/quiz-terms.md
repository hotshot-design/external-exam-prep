# Film quiz: terminology

Match terms to meanings. The terms come from the [glossary](/film/glossary).

:::quiz
question: Shots and framing: match each term to its meaning
pair: Extreme long shot (ELS) => Subject very small in a vast setting; establishes place or isolation.
pair: Long shot (LS) => Full body with surroundings visible.
pair: Medium shot (MS) => Waist-up; balances character and setting.
pair: Close-up (CU) => Face or object fills frame; shows emotion or significance.
pair: Extreme close-up (ECU) => Tiny detail such as eyes; intense focus.
:::

:::quiz
question: Sound: match each term to its meaning
pair: Diegetic sound => Sound that comes from inside the story world.
pair: Non-diegetic sound => Sound added for the audience (score, voice-over).
pair: Score => Original music composed for the film.
pair: Soundtrack => Collection of songs and music used.
pair: Voice-over => Narration not tied to an on-screen speaker.
:::

:::quiz
question: Editing: match each term to its meaning
pair: Continuity editing => Cutting that keeps time and space seamless and invisible.
pair: Montage => Series of shots edited to create meaning or compress time.
pair: Soviet montage => Editing that creates new meaning by juxtaposing shots (Vertov, Eisenstein).
pair: Jump cut => Cut within the same shot that skips time and is noticeable.
pair: Match cut => Cut linking two shots by similar action, shape or sound.
:::

:::quiz
question: Audiences and institutions: match each term to its meaning
pair: Target audience => The specific group (age, interests, needs) a product is made for.
pair: Demographic => Measurable audience characteristics such as age, gender, income, location.
pair: Audience engagement => How viewers respond: watch time, shares, likes, interaction.
pair: Audience positioning => How the text invites viewers to feel, align or respond.
pair: Institution => An organisation (studio, agency, broadcaster, platform, funder) shaping production and use.
pair: Commissioning => A client or organisation paying for a product to be made for a purpose.
:::

:::quiz
question: Representation and context: match each term to its meaning
pair: Representation => How people, places, events, ideas and emotions are constructed and portrayed.
pair: Stereotype => Oversimplified, fixed portrayal of a group.
pair: Ideology => System of values and beliefs promoted by a text.
pair: Audience positioning => How a text encourages viewers to see or judge things.
pair: Perspective / point of view => Whose view the story is told from.
:::
